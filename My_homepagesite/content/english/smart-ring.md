SmartRing is an AI-enhanced wearable interface for meetings, built around an nRF52840-based prototype. The project explores how a small wearable device can help people interact with computers and AI systems without reaching for a keyboard, mouse, or phone.

The prototype integrates IMU-based gesture control, BLE HID interaction, audio capture, embedded UI, and battery management. On the software side, I developed a real-time AI workflow using Google Gemini APIs to transform live speech into meeting summaries, key decisions, and action items for hands-free documentation.

The goal is to make meeting interaction more natural: gesture to control, speak to capture context, and let the AI produce structured notes while the user stays present in the conversation.
