Music has always been another language for me. Alongside engineering, I perform as a guitarist and Chinese traditional musician, using music as a way to connect with people, culture, and community.

My performance experience spans personal guitar practice, Chinese traditional music, and community cultural events. I enjoy the discipline of learning an instrument, but I also love the collaborative side of performance: listening closely, responding to other musicians, and turning practice into something that can be shared with an audience.

In North Carolina, I support cultural outreach through music performance and digital engagement. This work lets me combine my technical background with my creative life: building tools, helping with online presence, and participating in live performances that introduce Chinese traditional music to more people.

You can learn more about the ensemble and its community work here:
[NC Chinese Music Ensemble](https://ncchinesemusic.org/).
